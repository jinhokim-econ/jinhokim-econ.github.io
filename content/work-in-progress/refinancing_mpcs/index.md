abstract: >-
  The consumption effect of a rate cut depends on whether households that refinance quickly are the ones that spend the cash flow they gain. We plan to link the University of California Consumer Credit Panel to GSE loan performance data and HMDA, measure each borrower’s revealed refinancing responsiveness and spending response, and enter their covariance into a sufficient statistic that extends the redistribution channel of Auclert (2019) to refinancing.
